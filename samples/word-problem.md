## Level 5

**word-problem:5:5000**

Sara is 4 times as old as Tom. In 4 years, Sara will be twice as old as Tom. How old is Tom now?

A. 3  
B. 8  
C. 4  
D. 2 ✅  
E. 6

> Let Tom be x, so Sara is 4x. Then 4x + 4 = 2(x + 4), so 2x = 4 and x = 2. Check: 8 + 4 = 12 = 2 × 6.  
> _features: steps=5, valid=1_

---

**word-problem:5:5001**

A driver goes 360 km to a city at 60 km/h and returns the same way at 90 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip, in km/h?

A. 85  
B. 74  
C. 65  
D. 75  
E. 72 ✅

> Time: 360 ÷ 60 + 360 ÷ 90 = 6 + 4 = 10 hours for 720 km. 720 ÷ 10 = 72 km/h. (The plain average of the speeds, 75, ignores that the slower leg takes longer.)  
> _features: steps=5, valid=1_
